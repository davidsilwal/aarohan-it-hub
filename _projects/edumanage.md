A comprehensive cloud-based school management platform that streamlines student enrollment, attendance tracking, grade management, and parent-teacher communication. The system handles over 5,000 students and 200 staff members across multiple campuses.

### Key Features
- Student information management
- Automated attendance tracking
- Report card generation
- Parent-teacher communication portal
- Fee management system
- Library management
- Transport management
