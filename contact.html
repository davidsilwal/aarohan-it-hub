FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What engagement models do you support?

We work on both fixed-deliverable milestones (ideal for defined MVPs or migrations) and dedicated sprint retainers (for evolving platforms requiring ongoing senior engineering).

Who owns the intellectual property and code?

You own 100% of the code, architectures, database schemas, and assets we build. All repositories are transferred to your organization upon milestone completion.

Do you sign non-disclosure agreements (NDAs)?

Yes. We regularly execute mutual NDAs prior to reviewing proprietary specifications, internal codebases, or commercial data.

Can you audit or modernize existing software?

Absolutely. A substantial portion of our consulting involves auditing existing codebases, performance bottlenecks, cloud cost anomalies, and security vulnerabilities.
